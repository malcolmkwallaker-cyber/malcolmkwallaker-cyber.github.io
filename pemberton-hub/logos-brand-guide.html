Logos & Brand Guide.

The mark, the palette, the rules. Use these files; never rebuild or recolor the logo.

The mark

An asymmetrical infinity: the small rounded loop is the standard we hold, the large open loop is the reach we deliver. Small loop stays LEFT. Never flip it, never make it symmetrical, no shadows or effects.

Palette

  • Navy — the anchor ink#0F1B2D
  • Gold — accent only, never body text#C8A860
  • Cream — the signature ground#F0E9E1
  • Orange — tiny labels only#F0541C

Type & taglines
