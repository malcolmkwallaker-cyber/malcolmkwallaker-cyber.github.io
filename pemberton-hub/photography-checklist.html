Photography Checklist

Send this to your sellers before photo day. A prepared home photographs like a more expensive home.

Before the photographer arrives

  • Whole homeLights on, blinds open, fans off, pets away
  • KitchenCounters clear, no magnets on the fridge, hide dish soap
  • BathsToilet lids down, fresh towels, personal items off counters
  • BedroomsBeds made, floors clear, closet doors shut
  • ExteriorCars off the driveway, bins hidden, hose coiled, mow within 3 days
  • Lake / cabinDock cleared, boat staged or removed, firewood stacked neatly
  • SeasonalSnow: shovel paths; Summer: water the lawn the night before
Photographers stage light, not stuff — anything left out will be in the photos. When in doubt, put it away.
