The flag, the palette, the rules. Use these files; never rebuild, recolor or add effects to the mark.

The mark

A pennant flag: planted, not passing through. It reads two ways — the flag we plant on every deal we close, and the flag people rally to. Keep it simple: no shadows, no gradients, no redrawing.

  • flag-mark-white.svgTop bars, dark surfaces, watermarks
  • flag-badge-gold.svgFavicons, success screens, footer badge, avatars

Palette

  • Ink — the anchor. Nav, heroes, buttons, body text#0C0C0C
  • Gold — accents, labels, the second word. Never body text#C4A15A
  • Gold deep — gold that has to read on white#A8853F
  • Cream — soft section ground, chips, badges#F3F0EA
  • Line — thin card borders and dividers#E6E1D7
  • White — content cards and light sections#FFFFFF

Type & voice

  • Sans (everything)Manrope — 800 headlines, 700 labels, 400–500 body
  • Serif (prices, big numbers, dates)Playfair Display, italic for accents
  • Headline patternBold, sentence case, last word gold, trailing period
  • LabelsLetterspaced caps (0.2em+), gold
  • Lockupwhite "Steadfast" + gold "Real Estate Co."
  • Taglines (always this order)The Standard We Hold · The Reach We Deliver
