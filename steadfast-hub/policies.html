Commissions & Splits

Commission disbursements are processed within two business days of a compliant closed file. Splits follow your current agent agreement; cap progress is visible on your monthly agent bill. Bonuses (including agent-referral bonuses) post on the next bill cycle after the qualifying milestone.

Commission advances are available on pending files with all contingencies cleared — see the front desk for the advance request form.

File Compliance

Every transaction must be opened through Transaction Services within 48 hours of execution. A file is compliant when it contains the executed agreement, all addenda, agency disclosures, and earnest money documentation. Non-compliant files hold commission disbursement until cured.

Advertising & Sign Standards

All public-facing marketing uses the Steadfast brand system — black, gold and cream, correct logo orientation, no effects. Use the templates in Logos & Brand Guide. Yard signs are installed and removed by our sign vendor only; order through Listing Marketing.

Team names and personal brands may appear alongside, never instead of, the brokerage identity required by state rule.

Office Exclusives Policy

Office exclusives are shared internally on the Office Exclusives board before public marketing, consistent with the seller's signed instruction and MLS clear-cooperation rules. Do not advertise an office exclusive publicly in any channel, including social media teasers.

Referral Fees

Outgoing referrals must be papered on the company referral agreement before the client is introduced — use the Outgoing Referral flow. Incoming and outgoing referral fees are disclosed on the transaction file. Unwritten referral arrangements are not enforceable and not permitted.

Professional Conduct

We hold the standard: honest dealing, prompt communication, and respect for clients, colleagues and cooperating agents. Complaints are routed to the managing broker and handled directly. Fair housing law is a floor, not a ceiling — every client gets our full reach.

Office & Equipment

Office hours are 8:30–5:00 weekdays; agent key fobs work 24/7. Conference rooms book through the front desk calendar. Printing is unlimited for transaction documents; large-format marketing prints route through the brand desk.

Time Away & Coverage

Going out of town? Arrange a covering agent and notify your coordinator so active files never wait. The office maintains a coverage list of agents willing to host showings and inspections for a standard courtesy fee.
